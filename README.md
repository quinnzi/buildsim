# Potential of Potential Energy

I made this as a site to contain information about the different types of potential energies, with there being interactive demos to engage the reader. You can use your up and down arrow keys to change the gravitational potential energy of a bowling ball, a view a combustion engine in action. I made this because how energy works has always fascinated me. Every physical process in the universe involves energy in some way, yet energy is so unseen and how it's converted is so interesting to me. So, I thought I'd make a website hving to do with potential energy, the most unseen energy of all! I learned a lot making this and I'm glad I did :D
